## Current text

**Label:** A note from the founder
**Attribution:** Saleem — Founder
**Sign-off:** إن شاء الله

---

So many of us hit the same quiet roadblock. You're in the middle of getting to know
someone for marriage — the most important decision of your life — and you realise you
don't actually know *what to ask* to be sure this is half your deen.

So you start scraping together questions from Reddit threads, ChatGPT prompts, blog
posts written for nobody in particular. None of it is tailored to Muslims. None of it
asks the things that actually matter — the deen, the home, the way two lives quietly fit
together.

I built Taaruf to be the structured, Muslim-tailored conversation I wished I'd had: the
right questions, in the right order, with a mahram in the room and intention at the door.

---

## What's worth keeping

Written by the person who hid it, so you know what a rewrite would be trading away:

- **The Reddit-threads-and-ChatGPT-prompts line is the strongest thing on the page.** It
  is specific, slightly unflattering, and instantly recognisable to anyone who has been
  through it. Generic founder copy cannot buy that.
- **"You don't actually know what to ask"** is the whole product thesis in eight words.
- **The three-paragraph shape works**: the problem, why existing answers fail, what you
  built. Worth preserving even if every sentence changes.

## What needs to change

- **Tense.** "I built Taaruf to be…" reads as a thing that was made and is now finished
  — which is fine now that it is live, but the surrounding page used to say "launching
  soon". Check it reads right next to a live product with a "Start a session" button.
- **No call to action.** It ends on a sign-off. Now that the page sends people to the
  app, the note could hand off to that.
- **It never says it is free**, which the hero now does.

## Formatting notes

- Keep it to three or four short paragraphs — the layout is a two-column grid with the
  label and name on the left, and it starts to look unbalanced past about 180 words.
- *Italics* map to `<em>` in the HTML. Currently used only on *what to ask*.
- The Arabic sign-off (`إن شاء الله`) sits in its own styled element; keep or drop it,
  but tell me which.
- Do not add contact details here — the footer already carries them.
